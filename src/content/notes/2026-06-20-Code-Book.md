Another great book checked off the reading list: Code: The Hidden Language of Computer Hardware and Software.

It filled in so many gaps I didn't even realize I had about how computers actually work. Watching the book build from simple switches and electrical circuits all the way up to a complete computer was incredibly satisfying.

For the first time, concepts like logic gates, memory, and computation felt tangible from a physical and electrical perspective. Highly recommend it to anyone working in software who wants to better understand what's happening underneath the hood.
